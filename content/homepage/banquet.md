Established in 1966, Silver Dragon is located in the heart of Chinatown and is just a short walk from the seminar hotels.

**Your ticket includes** a full multi-course meal with wine, beer, non-alcoholic options, and dessert. This is the main event of our celebration. It wouldn't be the same without you. Guests are warmly welcome.
